Job Title: Data Scientist
Company: Bluepeak Retail Analytics (synthetic)
Location: Pune, India

About us
Bluepeak helps retailers understand their customers. Our team of data scientists builds the models behind demand forecasting, pricing and personalised recommendations.

What you will do
- Build, evaluate and deploy machine learning models for forecasting and recommendations.
- Analyse large datasets to find trends and turn them into clear business recommendations.
- Design and analyse experiments to measure the impact of product changes.
- Write clean, reusable code for data preparation and model inputs.
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders with clear visualisations.
- Work with data engineers to move models from notebooks into production pipelines.

Must have
- 3+ years of experience as a data scientist or machine learning engineer.
- Strong programming skills in Python, including pandas and scikit-learn.
- Proficient in SQL.
- Solid foundation in statistics and machine learning.
- Master's degree in Statistics, Computer Science, Mathematics or a related field.

Preferred
- Experience with deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Familiarity with Apache Spark for large-scale data processing.
- Experience with A/B testing.
- Knowledge of Tableau or Power BI is a bonus.
